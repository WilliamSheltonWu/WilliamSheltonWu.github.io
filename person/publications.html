Research Output 论文发表及研究成果

Smoke Cover Model Based on Particle Swarm and Slime Mold Hybrid Algorithm

基于粒子群与黏菌混合算法的烟雾遮盖模型

CUMCM 2025
CUMCM 2025

This paper constructs a smoke coverage model based on a hybrid optimization strategy combining particle swarm optimization and slime mold algorithm, so as to improve the simulation accuracy and adaptability of smoke diffusion and coverage effects.

本文基于粒子群与黏菌算法的混合优化策略，构建烟雾遮盖模型，提升烟雾扩散与遮盖效果的模拟精度与适配性。
